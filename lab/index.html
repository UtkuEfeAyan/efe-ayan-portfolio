Interactive Mini-Lab

A fully functional in-browser procedural language generator — plus a teaser for a text-based adventure.

conlang.exe — procedural lexicon

How it works

Syllable grammars shift with theme: earthlike inventories, fantasy clusters, or alien stacks. Click any generated word to copy it. Meanings are randomized flavor text for prototyping lore.

Tip: crank complexity + alien theme for denser phonotactics.

Ship this elsewhere

Embed the same generator in a game UI, Twine passage, or research notebook. Source lives in js/language-gen.js.
