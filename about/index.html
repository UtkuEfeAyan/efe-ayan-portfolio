About Me

I’m Efe Ayan — a game designer, creative coder, and AI researcher studying Computer Science & Game Design at UC Santa Cruz. I care about systems that feel playful and precise: procedural languages, adaptive audio, generative visuals, and tools that help teams prototype faster.

Outside of class builds I’m usually deep in lore rabbit holes, TouchDesigner experiments, or D&D worldbuilding — which is exactly why tools like Voices of Void exist.
