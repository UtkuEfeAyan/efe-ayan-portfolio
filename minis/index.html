GLSLShadertoyGenerative

Whirlpool Sun

A swirling star over a procedural galaxy. Drag to heat the core and grow the sphere.

Play → Shadertoy

About

Final Shadertoy assignment: a whirlpool-shaded sun sitting in a starfield, with mouse-driven heat (X) and radius (Y). Inspired by Yohei Nishitsuji’s whirlpool sketch, then remixed with a galaxy backdrop and bloom.

Technical

Self-hosted WebGL2 player with Shadertoy uniforms (iTime, iMouse, iResolution). The Image pass is the original GLSL from WfcyWS; nebula input is generated locally so it runs without Shadertoy’s texture CDN.

p5.jsGenerativeImages

Circle Image Generator

Pick an inspiration image and watch colored dots evolve into the picture via a pointillist mutation algorithm.

Play → Repo

About

An evolutionary / pointillist recreation toy. Seed translucent circles sampled from image colors, then mutate and grow the cloud until the subject emerges.

Technical

p5.js canvas with tunable mutation rate, circle counts, radius, and alpha. Score tracks pixel error vs the inspiration image.

p5.jsWorldPCG

Animal World

Isometric playfield that expands from the center: hunt animals, avoid packs, chase a score goal.

Play →

About

A soft hunting / survival loop on a hashed procedural board. Tiles reveal outward from the middle in square rings so the whole field fills in.

Technical

XXHash-seeded spawns, animal levels/movement toward the player, tree blockers, and local sprite assets. Camera tracks the player each frame.
