Procedural Tileset

About

Technical

p5.jsWorld

Animal World

Isometric playfield that expands from the center; hunt animals, avoid packs, chase a score goal.

Play

About

A soft hunting / survival loop on a hashed procedural board. Tiles reveal outward from the middle in square rings so the whole field fills in.

Technical

XXHash-seeded spawns, animal levels/movement toward the player, tree blockers, and local sprite assets. Camera tracks the player each frame.

PhaserGame

Flappy Bird Clone

Tap to flap — pipes with randomised spacing, difficulty ramps, and late-game rockets.

Play Repo

About

A Flappy-style endless flyer with score levels, moving pipes, and rocket hazards. Built as a compact playable mini.

Technical

Phaser 3, custom graphics, Web Audio beeps. Pipe gaps use distance-based spawn: minimum spacing matches the original pace, randomised up to 2× for variety. First pipes appear near the bird so wide screens don’t wait.
