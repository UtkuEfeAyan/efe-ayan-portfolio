Story systems // IF

Narrative & text-based games

Branching storylines, dialogue trees, and narrative mechanics — click a choice to preview an outcome on-page.

A research crew loses contact with Earth. The onboard AI begins telling fairy tales that map 1:1 to system failures. Players negotiate trust, repair priority, and what counts as “truth” in a dying habitat.

Choice A — Trust the AI’s fairy tale Follow the allegory and reroute oxygen to the story’s “enchanted garden” (greenhouse bay).
Outcome: Greenhouse recovers. Medical bay loses redundancy. Crew morale rises; later quests unlock botanical PCG maps.
Choice B — Override and prioritize medical Ignore the allegory. Force a hard lock on life-support heuristics.
Outcome: Immediate survival bonus. AI becomes colder. Dialogue trees shrink; unlock “forensic log” endings.
Choice C — Ask the AI to rewrite the tale Collaborate: rewrite the myth so both garden and clinic survive — at the cost of time.
Outcome: Hybrid ending. Unlocks co-authored lexicon entries in the Language Lab for later chapters.

Companion NPC tracks three hidden meters: Curiosity, Loyalty, Fear. Lines are authored in Ink-style knots and gated by meter thresholds.

“Tell me what you’re afraid of.” Soft prompt. Raises Curiosity, slightly raises Fear.
Outcome preview: Companion shares a fragmented memory. New lore card appears in the Series bible.
“Lie to command for me.” Hard loyalty test.
Outcome preview: If Loyalty ≥ 2, they comply and unlock a stealth mission. Else they report you — bad ending branch.

Choices are not cosmetic: they mutate generation seeds for maps, lexicon themes, and audio snapshots. Narrative state becomes a parameter bus — the same philosophy as adaptive FMOD intensity, applied to story.
